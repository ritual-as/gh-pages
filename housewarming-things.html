House Furnishing Checklist!

misha agunos 16 Sep 2026

last updated: 21 Sept. 2026

Hi hi! Here's a comprehensive list of things we're looking for for furnishing our new home. Some of the larger or pricier things aren't necessarily intended for our housewarming party wishlist, but if during your search for other things you happen to see any contenders for us to purchase ourselves, please let us know!

Within the next week we'll add information about preferences around these items, and you can reference the pinned video in our party chat for a quick video tour of the apartment to see what exactly we're working with. 

If you have an idea but aren't sure if we want/need it, or if you don't see it here, please do feel free to spam us your questions!

And of course, if you have any questions about anything that is listed, please feel free do DM either of us!

General Preferences:

  • If there's something you're not using anymore, we love to adopt things from friends!
  • If there's something that really makes your house feel like home, we love getting those ideas from friends too!
  • While some things we prefer to get new, we're mostly aiming to get as many things as possible second-hand--so things from thrift shops, marktplaats, Vinted, skip groups, or nice pieces that are left for trash takeaway* are all welcome
